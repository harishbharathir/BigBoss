Research Pillars & Architectural Novelty

1. Multimodal Open-Vocabulary Grounding Couples YOLO-World bounding box proposals with 768-dim SigLIP vision-language embeddings, overcoming closed-vocabulary detector limitations.
2. Grounded Evidence Traceability Every query response resolves strictly to verified camera feeds, exact timestamps, and high-fidelity visual evidence crops.
3. Clarify-Once Persistent Spatial Memory Ambiguous spatial referents (main gate, rear exit) trigger a one-time clarification persisted to disk across restarts.
4. Cross-Camera Re-ID & Handover Delay Temporal graph association links disconnected single-camera tracklets into cohesive multi-camera vehicle journeys with precise handover latency (Δt).
